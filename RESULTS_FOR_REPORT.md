# Defensible results for the report or article

The strongest new result is **model-parameter and checkpoint compression through actual teacher–student feature distillation**. The 12-layer teacher had 117.3 million parameters; the 6-layer student had 74.8 million, a 36.3% reduction. On 19 held-out Indian-English lecture clips, mean cosine similarity between the student's and teacher's global hidden states increased from 0.222 before distillation to 0.937 after three epochs (432 updates on 144 training clips); mean feature loss decreased from 1.722 to 0.283. When both weight files were saved at bfloat16 precision, the student's weights occupied 149.6 MB versus 234.7 MB for the teacher, a 36.3% reduction. The student generated nonempty WAV files for all 24 fixed payment prompts. These are reproducible **structural and feature-matching** findings, not evidence of better perceived speech or reduced runtime memory.
