# Local validation run — 22 October 2026

The source checkpoints were the local original MOSS-TTS-Nano and the final three-epoch Indian-English fine-tuning checkpoint. `asset_hashes.json` records their exact weights. The MOSS audio-tokenizer checkpoint was shared by both systems.

The 24 `split=test` payment prompts were generated once per checkpoint after one unmeasured warm-up. Runs used Apple Silicon MPS, seed 42, `max_new_frames=160`, and separate Python processes. The original and adapted measurements are paired by `sample_id` in the two CSV files. `inference_seconds` is wall-clock model inference; `rtf` is inference time divided by generated WAV duration. Sampled RSS includes the whole process and codec. Audio files are regenerated by the benchmark and are not included in Git.

Mean RTF: 0.876 original, 0.976 adapted. Some individual RTF values exceed 1. The adapted checkpoint did not improve latency. These results do not establish better speech quality or model compression.
